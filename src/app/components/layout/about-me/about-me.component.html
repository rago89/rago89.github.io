About me

I am a Civil Engineer turned web developer. After years of working as an Engineer and a period as a salesman, my life underwent a significant transformation. Now, I’m here in Belgium, and as part of that change, I decided to shift my professional path by enrolling in Hack Your Future Belgium. What started as a curiosity about coding has grown into a deep passion.

Currently, I am working as a web developer at Fedasil, the Belgian federal agency responsible for asylum seekers. In my role, I utilize modern technologies such as GraphQL, Hasura, Keycloak, Docker, and Jira to create efficient and constructive solutions. I am passionate about learning new things and believe that an open mind fosters creativity and the ability to draw inspiration from various fields to implement in my work.
